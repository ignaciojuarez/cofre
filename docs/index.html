Zero to playing

Four steps. One button each.

  2. 2

    Step 2 · On your iPhone

    Turn on Developer Mode

    2 min
    1. Open Settings → Privacy & Security.
    2. Scroll to Security, tap Developer Mode, turn it on.
    3. Tap Restart. After it restarts, unlock, tap Turn On (or Enable) and enter your passcode.
    Don't see it?

    iOS hides Developer Mode until the iPhone has been paired with a Mac. Do Step 3 first, then come back here.

    Opening Cofre and seeing “Developer Mode Required”? Same fix: finish this step.

  3. 3

    Step 3 · On a Mac, once

    Connect to a Mac

    2 min
    1. Have Cofre installed on your iPhone (Step 1).
    2. Plug your iPhone into the Mac with a cable.
    3. Open Terminal (press ⌘ Space, type “Terminal”, press Return). Paste the command below and press Return.
    4. A Cofre window opens and guides you: unlock your iPhone, tap Trust, then wait for Done.
    5. Open Cofre on your iPhone. It's ready.
    Turn on JavaScript to see the command.

    Never share your pairing key

    Never paste or upload your pairing file to any chat, website or person. Not even ChatGPT or Claude. It's a key to your phone.

    If the window shows “Send with AirDrop”
    1. Click Send with AirDrop and pick your iPhone. On the iPhone, tap Accept. It's saved in Files → Downloads.
    2. Open Cofre, tap I have a file → Downloads → the file named after your iPhone.
    What the command does

    Shortcut · In person

    No Mac? Use a friend's. Plug into their Mac, paste the command, tap Trust. About 2 minutes, and nothing stays on their Mac.

  4. 4

    Step 4 · On your iPhone

    Open Cofre and play

    3 min
    1. Open Cofre. Your key is already inside, so setup moves on by itself.
    2. When iOS asks to add a VPN, tap Allow. Cofre then tests JIT for you.
    3. Tap Sign in with Microsoft, then Play.

    From then on, just tap Play. JIT and the VPN turn on by themselves.

Before you start

What you need

  • iPhone or iPad

    iOS 26. Tested on iPhone 17 Pro with iOS 26. Other iPhones and iPads on iOS 26 should work but aren't tested yet. Wait for our OK before big iOS updates.

  • Minecraft: Java Edition

    A Microsoft account that owns Java Edition. Minecraft bought on a phone or console doesn't include it.

  • A Mac, just once

    For Step 3: about 2 minutes, plus a cable for your iPhone. A friend's Mac works too. Windows & Linux: coming soon

  • Free space and Wi-Fi

    Keep about 8 GB free. Cofre is about 760 MB. Each modpack takes 1–6 GB. Wi-Fi is needed to turn on JIT.

Straight answers

What works, and the limits

Works

  • Play with PC friends. It's real Java Edition, so you join the same servers as PC players. Bedrock players (consoles, phones) can join only if the server runs Geyser.
  • Modded servers. Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge. Install the server's modpack or the same mods from Modrinth or CurseForge.
  • Coming: one-tap Join. Servers will be able to publish a card so Cofre installs the right mods and joins for you.

Limits

  • Memory. Phones have less RAM than PCs. Cofre picks a safe amount for you. On 6 GB iPhones keep packs to about 150 mods, and lower the resolution if it closes.
  • PC-only mods. Mods with desktop native code don't work, such as Simple Voice Chat, Plasmo Voice and Sound Physics Remastered.
  • iOS updates can break JIT. Turn off automatic updates and wait for our OK.

FAQ & troubleshooting

Help

JIT won't turn on
  1. Check Developer Mode is on (Step 2).
  2. Use Wi-Fi, and turn off any other VPN (work, ad blocker). iOS runs one VPN at a time.
  3. Right after a restart, JIT needs internet once to set up.
  4. Close Cofre fully and open it again. Then try Settings → JIT & Java → Test JIT.
  5. Still failing? Your pairing key may be stale. See the next answer.
It stopped working after an iOS update or a reset

An iOS update, erasing the iPhone, or Reset Location & Privacy can make your pairing key stop working. Redo Step 3: plug in and run the command again (2 minutes).

To avoid it: Settings → General → Software Update → Automatic Updates, turn them off.

Why does Cofre ask to add a VPN?

JIT needs Cofre to talk to your own iPhone. The VPN is local: it only carries that one connection inside the phone. Your browsing doesn't go through it. Tap Allow and enter your passcode.

“Not enough storage”

Keep about 8 GB free. Delete instances you don't play in Instances, and check Settings → General → iPhone Storage.

Can I share my pairing file?

No. Never paste or upload it to any chat, website or person, not even ChatGPT or Claude. It's a key to your iPhone: anyone who has it can control your phone like a trusted computer. It also only works for the iPhone it was made for. Everyone runs Step 3 for their own phone.

Is the Terminal command safe?

It downloads a small helper signed by us into a temporary folder, opens it, puts the key inside Cofre over the cable, and deletes itself. Nothing is installed or uploaded, and it never asks for a password. Read exactly what it does.

Does it work on cellular data?

Turning on JIT needs Wi-Fi. With no Wi-Fi around, you can still play single-player: start Cofre, then turn on Airplane Mode.

How long does Cofre last?

Invite installs last 1 year. Cofre warns you 30 days before. Installing the new link keeps your worlds. Sideloaded with a free Apple ID: 7 days, then refresh.

Privacy & safety

Your key stays with you

  • Your pairing key never leaves your devices

    It goes from the Mac straight into Cofre over the cable. Cofre keeps it on the phone. It's never uploaded or logged.

  • The helper deletes itself

    It runs from a temporary folder and is gone when it says Done. Nothing installed, no password, no account.

  • Local VPN, Microsoft sign-in

    The VPN never leaves your phone. You sign in on Microsoft's own page.

  • Open source

    Cofre is GPL-3.0. Read every line, or build it yourself.
